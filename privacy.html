Fönster — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

What we collect

Fönster stores the sleep, feeding and nappy events you log, your baby's date of birth, and the optional names you enter for the baby and caregivers. There are no accounts to create, and we do not ask for an email address or phone number.

To operate and improve the app, we also collect limited product-usage events (for example, app open, onboarding completed, a sleep logged, a sleep window viewed, paywall viewed, purchase started or completed, and an invitation shared), subscription status, install and campaign attribution, and technical information such as app version, operating system, device model, locale, an automatically generated app or device identifier, and coarse location inferred from the network address. Product-usage events do not include baby names, caregiver names, dates of birth, or the contents and timestamps of care records.

Where it lives

Your data is stored on your device first, and is also synchronised to our server (hosted on Supabase, USA) so that a second caregiver can join with a link and both phones show the same log. Records are keyed by random identifiers, not by your identity — we could not tell who a household belongs to even if asked.

Analytics, attribution and subscriptions

We use PostHog for anonymous product analytics, AppsFlyer for install, referral and advertising-campaign attribution, and Superwall for paywall and subscription analytics. These service providers process the limited usage, purchase, attribution, device and network information described above on our behalf. AppsFlyer may use the Identifier for Vendor (IDFV); Fönster does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not receive access to Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA).

We do not sell personal data or show third-party ads. Attribution data is used to measure Fönster's own marketing, not to personalise third-party advertising. We never take old data hostage: export (CSV) works even without a subscription.

Deletion

Deleting the app removes local data. To request deletion of synced household data and associated analytics or attribution records we can identify, email us with your invite code or household details: next.epic.dev@gmail.com.

Payments

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Not medical advice

Fönster gives general guidance based on age and your own logs. It is not medical advice — talk to your child health service (BVC, sundhedsplejerske, neuvola, health visitor) or a doctor if you are worried.
